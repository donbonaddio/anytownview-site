Privacy Policy

Anytownview · Last updated November 2026

Anytownview is a town-tour app for iPhone and iPad. There is no Anytownview account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or photos, and we never receive anything about you or how you use it.

What the app loads, and from where

Town guides, facts and maps come with the app. To show photos, videos, live views and weather, your device fetches public content directly from these services. Like any website you visit, each service sees your device’s IP address and the item requested. Anytownview sends them nothing else: no name, no account and no device identifier.

  • Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org) for town photos.
  • YouTube for tour videos and live views, which play in YouTube’s own embedded player, and for video thumbnails (i.ytimg.com). YouTube’s player is covered by Google’s privacy policy.
  • Public traffic cameras, such as Caltrans in California, for still and live camera views in some towns.
  • Weather: the U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) for US towns and Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) elsewhere. These requests carry the town’s location, never yours.
  • NYC Open Data for public events in New York.

Links such as “Read more on Wikipedia” or “View on Wikimedia Commons” open those sites in your browser, under their own policies.

What stays on your device

Your settings, such as the town you last opened and the views you picked, are saved on your device only. Deleting the app removes them. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Anytownview is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
